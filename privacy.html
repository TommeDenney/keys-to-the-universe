Keys to the Universe

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Keys to the Universe is a free, open-source piano learning app made by an independent developer. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no server of its own. The developer never receives your data.

What stays on your device

  • Progress and settings (lessons, Stardust, items, your explorer avatar, and the profile you enter) are stored in your browser's local storage.
  • Microphone audio is analysed on your device in real time to hear which notes you play. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • MIDI input from a connected piano is handled on your device only.
  • Imported sheet music, private packs and photos stay on your device and are never uploaded.

Signing in with Google (optional)

You can play without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks Google for:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture (the openid, email and profile permissions), used to show who is signed in and to greet you.
  • Access to files the app creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). The app makes a “Keys to the Universe” folder and saves your progress there as progress.json. It cannot see, read or change any other files in your Drive.

Your progress and the profile you enter (display name, birthday, optional birth year and piano experience) are saved only in that folder in your own Drive. Your birthday is used only for a once-a-year Stardust gift. Information goes directly between your device and Google; it does not pass through any server run by the developer.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never used to train AI models.

If you choose “Remember this device”, a sign-in token is kept in your browser for up to 30 days so you don't need to sign in each time.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: Settings › Reset progress clears your lessons and stars; clearing this website's data in your browser removes everything.
  • In Google Drive: sign out in Settings, delete the “Keys to the Universe” folder from your Drive, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Keys to the Universe is made for learners of all ages, and parental discretion is advised. Children can use it fully without an account, and in that case no personal information leaves the device. Google accounts are for ages 13 and up unless a parent manages the account through Family Link. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Cookies, analytics and third parties

The app uses no cookies for tracking, no analytics and no advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Signing in uses Google's services under Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
